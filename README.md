# Paid Flexibility vs AI Rebooking

Synthetic B2B hotel pricing case. A 90-day pilot plan for a travel platform: sell flexibility as a paid product, keep deep discounts in a closed channel, and measure the effect before a full rollout.

All figures are modelled. Nothing here is proprietary company data.

**Open the memo:** [index.html](index.html) · [PDF](pilot-plan.pdf)

Language switch: RU / EN in the top right of the page.

## What the case covers

- Business problem: free cancellation becomes expensive when AI agents rebook on every rate drop
- Proposed solution: firm rate as base price; flexibility premium by lead time (4% / 8% / 11%); closed-channel deep discount
- Economics on a synthetic book of €133.2m GBV: +€0.50m quarterly net revenue to prove in the pilot, up to +€1.40m on rollout, €2.10m of current revenue to protect
- Pilot rules, stop criteria, risks, and recommendations for adjacent teams
